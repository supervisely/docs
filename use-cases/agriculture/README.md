# Agriculture

Field images of crops are hard to annotate: the objects of interest are small, numerous, and partly hidden by leaves and neighboring plants. The use cases in this section show how to build training datasets for agricultural computer vision in Supervisely.

* [Wheat Head Segmentation with Live Training](wheat-head-segmentation.md): annotate wheat head masks in top-down field images and let Live Training – Segmentation pre-label the rest of the dataset.
